# Kinetic intelligence

This replaces the rejected avatar-film direction under the owner's explicit research → plan → build instruction.

## Metaphor and feel
Separate models, interfaces and systems become one useful mechanism. A machined orbital sculpture unfolds into a project exhibition. Precise, spatial, assured.

## Hero archetype and secondary moments
(c) Real-time Three.js object: original geometric sculpture, local studio lighting, scroll-driven separation, rotation and camera distance. No avatar, video or loading gate.

Secondary moments: horizontal project exhibition driven by vertical scroll on wide screens, project-plane depth, word statement reveal and large contact typography. Content stacks below 801px; reduced motion removes pinning/scrubbing.

## Motion signature
Power3.out arrivals; hero scrub 0.8; gallery scrub 0.75; Lenis lerp 0.085 on desktop. One GSAP ticker drives scroll and WebGL. The signature interaction is the orbital instrument unfolding as the identity makes way for the work. Pause control and offscreen/hidden-document suspension are implemented.
