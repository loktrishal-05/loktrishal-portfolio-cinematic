## Direction contract

**THESIS.** Intelligence as a kinetic instrument: an original machined sculpture assembles, rotates and unfolds with the visitor's scroll, carrying the page into an exhibition of actual work. A video with text beside it is explicitly rejected.

**OWN-WORLD.** Cool porcelain #ECEEF1, graphite #20252B, cobalt #2955DD, satin aluminium and physical highlights. Oversized Bricolage lettering, quiet Source Sans reading text, large media planes and precise controls.

**STORY.** Meet Loktrishal → see models, interfaces and systems connect → inspect six featured projects → browse the complete project index, expertise and sourced recognition → contact.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** The full-width name is a typographic backdrop. A large metallic orbital sculpture occupies the centre-right and overlaps the lettering spatially. A concise statement and work action sit in the clear lower-left. The navigation remains available; nothing waits for an intro video.

**FORM.** A pinned three-beat WebGL opening, a vertically driven horizontal project exhibition on wide screens, a scroll-revealed statement, restrained project depth and a typographic contact finale. On narrow/reduced-motion views the content stacks and remains readable. This is code-led execution under the owner's explicit research-plan-build instruction, not an approval waiting room.

**FINISH.** Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with browser checks, visual confirmation, DESIGN.md and provenance for shipping images. Any worker usage-limit failure is disclosed and completed directly, never labelled an independent review.
